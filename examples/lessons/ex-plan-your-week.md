Short, spaced sessions beat one long cram. A simple weekly plan makes that automatic.

## The plan
- **4-5 sessions** of 25-45 minutes, spread over the week.
- Each session starts with 5 minutes of **active recall** on the previous session.
- One slot per week for a **review** of everything so far.

## Try it
Write your plan for next week: days, times, and what you will study in each session.

```quiz
question: Which plan uses **spacing** best?
A: One 4-hour session on Sunday
B: Four 40-minute sessions on different days
C: Two 2-hour sessions back to back

explain: Spreading the same time over several days gives your memory time to consolidate between sessions.
```
